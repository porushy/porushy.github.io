Python · pandas · NumPy · scikit-learn · yfinance · Jupyter

- Engineered an unsupervised anomaly-detection pipeline using Isolation Forest to monitor 5,800+ days of USD–INR exchange-rate data for abnormal market behavior.
- Extracted log returns and rolling-volatility features, successfully mapping 74.3% of flagged anomalies directly to known macroeconomic crises (2008 GFC, 2013 taper tantrum, 2020 COVID).
- Developed a quantitative Market Stress Index intended for policy analysis, capturing a 5.09× baseline volatility spike during peak historical stress events.
